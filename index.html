User guide

A practical walkthrough for shoppers and store staff, from first launch to food rescue and donation tracking.

Explore interactive prototype ↗
Demo environment. Use fictional account details or the built-in demo account. Payments, stock integration, code scanning and charity notifications are simulated. No actual food orders or donations are placed.

01 / For shoppers

1
Open the app. Three short screens explain what FairShare does. Tap Next, or Skip if you are in a hurry.
2
Say how you will use it. Choose "Shop rescued food nearby" and tap Continue.
3
Create an account or log in. New here? Tap Sign up, type your first name, email and a password of at least 8 characters, tick the box, and tap Create account. Already have one? Type your email and password and tap Log in. Trying it out? Tap Fill in next to the demo account and then Log in.
4
Look at what is on offer. The Home screen lists food from nearby shops, cheapest-to-rescue first. Each card shows a photo, the shop, the pickup time and the price. Type in the search box or tap a filter such as Bakery, Vegan or Exact contents to narrow it down.
5
Open an offer to check it. Tap a card. You will see photos, what is inside (either the exact items or what the bag usually contains), how much food it is, where it came from, how it was stored and when it is best eaten. Two times are shown separately: when to pick it up, and the last moment you can cancel for free.
6
Reserve it. Choose how many you want and tap Reserve. Pick Pay now in the app or Pay at pickup, keep the reminder on if you want a nudge 30 minutes before, and tap Confirm reservation.
7
Keep your pickup code. The app shows a code such as FS-4821 and a QR code. You will find it again under Reservations. From here you can get Directions or add the pickup to your calendar.
8
Go to the shop and show the code. Staff scan it and hand you the food. Tap "I have picked it up" in the app.
9
Changed your mind? Open the reservation and tap Cancel before the cancellation deadline. If you paid in the app you get your money back, and the food goes back on sale.
10
See what you have done. The Impact tab adds up the kilos you rescued, the money you saved and the meals that represents. Tap "How the food journey works" to see what happened to food nobody bought and which charity received it.
11
Set it up your way. In Profile you can set your household size (so the app tells you if a bag is enough for you), turn reminders on or off, add food alerts such as "yoghurt", and log out.

02 / For store staff

1
Open the app and choose "Manage store surplus". Log in as usual. You can also switch to this view later from Profile.
2
Tap Sync. The app pulls in the items your stock system has flagged as close to their date, blemished or overstocked.
3
Decide what happens to each item. For every item choose Sell or Donate. Not sure? The app suggests one. Tap "Apply all suggestions" to deal with the whole list at once.
4
Add anything the system missed. Tap "Add an item manually", scan the barcode or type the name and weight, choose a reason, and add it.
5
Hand food to customers. When a shopper arrives, open Pickups and scan their code (or type it). The app confirms the handover.
6
Send the rest to a charity. Open Donations, tap New donation, pick one of the checked charity partners and tap Assign. The charity is told automatically. As the donation moves along, tap Advance so shoppers can see it was collected and delivered.
7
Show your impact. The Impact tab totals the kilos donated and the meals supported. Turn on "Show impact to shoppers" so customers see it too.

03 / Reset or switch roles

Open Profile to switch between shopper and store staff views, log out or reset prototype data. Reset returns the browser demo to its starting state.
